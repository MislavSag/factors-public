# US Equity Factors — public data

This is a noncommercial research edition. Returns are stored as decimals with normalized date labels.
Only the frequencies and series listed in the public catalog are included.

## Kenneth French

Download the original Fama–French series from the publisher.
[Publisher](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/Ken.French/data_library.html)

## Jensen–Kelly–Pedersen

US characteristics, themes, and size segments. Daily coverage includes the RiskPrism comparison factors.
Jensen, T. I., Kelly, B., and Pedersen, L. H. (2023), Is There a Replication Crisis in Finance?, Journal of Finance.
[Publisher](https://jkpfactors.com/data) · [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)

## Open Source Asset Pricing

Monthly US long–short portfolios from Chen and Zimmermann.
Chen, A. Y., and Zimmermann, T. (2022), Open Source Cross-Sectional Asset Pricing, Critical Finance Review. October 2026 release.
[Publisher](https://www.openassetpricing.com/data/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Tidy Finance

Monthly returns for selected portfolio-sort specifications.
Tidy Finance, Factor Library Portfolio Returns.
[Publisher](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tidy-finance/factor-library) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

## RiskPrism

US market, style, and industry coefficients estimated with cross-sectional WLS.
RiskPrism model artifacts. The monthly series are derived locally by compounding daily coefficients over complete NYSE trading months.
[Publisher](https://github.com/wanxinwanxin/risk-prism) · [Public derived model artifacts](https://github.com/wanxinwanxin/risk-prism/blob/main/docs/DECISIONS.md)
